# Privacy Policy for Funny Jokes & More Chrome Extension

## Last Updated: January 2024

### Information We Collect
**We collect NO personal information whatsoever.**

This extension:
- ✅ Does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data
- ✅ Does NOT track user behavior or usage patterns  
- ✅ Does NOT access your browsing history
- ✅ Does NOT require user accounts or login
- ✅ Does NOT use cookies or local storage for tracking
- ✅ Works completely offline after installation

### Data Usage
Since we don't collect any data, we don't use, share, or sell any data.

### Permissions Explanation
This extension requests minimal permissions:
- **activeTab**: Only to function as a browser extension popup (standard requirement)
- **No network permissions**: Extension works offline with pre-loaded jokes

### External Services
This extension does NOT communicate with any external servers or third-party services. All jokes are stored locally within the extension.

### Changes to Privacy Policy
Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in this document with an updated date.

### Contact Information
For any privacy-related questions, please create an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/AkshatNaruka/Dad-jokes-chrome-extension/issues).
